Meet Brandie

Brandie Ryan is the owner and stylist at Looking Glass Salon in Sterling, VA. Color is her specialty, especially color correction: bringing hair that has gone brassy, orange, patchy or flat back to a shade you love. She also does highlights, balayage, cuts, formaldehyde-free keratin smoothing and hair extensions.
